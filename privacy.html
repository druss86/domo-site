Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

1. Overview

This policy explains how the DOMO uploader app ("the App"), operated by DOMO, handles information when connected to TikTok. The App lets a creator upload their own videos to their TikTok inbox, where they finish editing and posting inside the TikTok app. The App does not publish posts directly.

2. Information accessed via TikTok

When you authorize the App using TikTok Login Kit, the App may access, only within the scopes you grant:

  • Basic profile info (e.g. open ID, display name, avatar) via user.info.basic, used to confirm which account is connected.
  • Permission to upload videos via video.upload, used to send a video you select to your TikTok inbox as a draft for you to finish in TikTok.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens, used only to make API requests on your behalf.

The App does not access your messages, contacts, followers' data, or any content other than what you choose to post.

3. Video uploads

The video file you select is sent to TikTok only after you click "Upload to TikTok inbox". It arrives in your TikTok inbox notifications; nothing is published until you finish and post it yourself in the TikTok app. The App does not store your video files.

4. How information is used

Only to sign you in, show which TikTok account is connected, upload the videos you choose, and show upload status. No advertising, profiling, or analytics resale.

5. No selling or sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data. Data is shared only with TikTok as required to perform uploads, or if required by law.

6. Retention

Your access token is kept only in your browser session and is cleared when you disconnect or close the tab; the App does not store refresh tokens or video files on its servers. Minimal technical logs (e.g. upload IDs, timestamps) may be kept by our hosting provider for up to 90 days for troubleshooting.

7. Revoking access & deletion requests

You may revoke the App's access anytime in TikTok settings (Security & permissions → Apps and services). To request deletion of any data the App holds, email russonthecusp@gmail.com; requests are completed within 30 days.

8. Security

Reasonable measures (encrypted transport, restricted token storage) protect data, though no method is 100% secure.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required by TikTok in your region).

10. Changes

Updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

11. Contact
