Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

1. Overview

This policy explains how the DOMO posting app ("the App"), operated by DOMO, handles information when connected to TikTok. The App exists solely to upload and publish DOMO's own videos to his own TikTok account.

2. Information accessed via TikTok

When you authorize the App using TikTok Login Kit, the App may access, only within the scopes you grant:

  • Basic profile info (e.g. open ID, display name, avatar) via user.info.basic, used to confirm which account is connected.
  • Permission to upload and/or publish videos via video.upload / video.publish, used to send videos you select to your TikTok account.
  • Creator info returned by the Content Posting API (e.g. allowed privacy levels, posting limits) needed to post correctly.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens, used only to make API requests on your behalf.

The App does not access your messages, contacts, followers' data, or any content other than what you choose to post.

3. Video uploads

Video files and captions you select are transmitted to TikTok solely to create the post. Files may be held temporarily during upload and are deleted afterward. Posting only happens with your action and settings.

4. How information is used

Only to authenticate, upload/publish videos, and show posting status. No advertising, profiling, or analytics resale.

5. No selling or sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data. Data is shared only with TikTok as required to perform posting, or if required by law.

6. Retention

Access tokens are stored securely and retained only while the App is authorized. Temporary video files are deleted after upload completes. Minimal logs (e.g. post IDs, timestamps) may be kept up to 90 days for troubleshooting.

7. Revoking access & deletion requests

You may revoke the App's access anytime in TikTok settings (Security & permissions → Apps and services). To request deletion of any data the App holds, email [CONTACT EMAIL]; requests are completed within 30 days.

8. Security

Reasonable measures (encrypted transport, restricted token storage) protect data, though no method is 100% secure.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required by TikTok in your region).

Updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
